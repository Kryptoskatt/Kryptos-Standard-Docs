The Kryptos plugin brings your crypto portfolio, transaction history, tax figures and accounting ledger
into ChatGPT. Install it once, choose the workspace ChatGPT may use, and ask questions in plain English.
The answers come straight from your Kryptos data.

:::info The plugin never moves funds
No tool can send, swap, withdraw, deposit or trade any asset. You sign in to Kryptos with OAuth, and
Kryptos never asks for exchange API keys or wallet private keys in the conversation.
:::

## Before you start

- A [Kryptos](https://kryptos.io) workspace on a paid plan: a **Pro** membership or a paid **Enterprise**
  subscription. Free accounts, the 14-day Enterprise trial and one-off tax-year reports don't qualify.
- A ChatGPT account

## Set it up in 7 steps

### 1. Open Plugins

In the ChatGPT sidebar, click **Plugins**.

### 2. Search for Kryptos

Type **Kryptos** into the plugin search and open the result *Kryptos — By Kryptos · Crypto portfolio,
tax & books*.

### 3. Install the plugin

On the Kryptos plugin page, click **Install plugin**. A Kryptos sign-in page opens.

### 4. Choose your workspace

Sign in to Kryptos as you normally would, then pick the **workspace** ChatGPT can use. Choose one on a
Pro or paid Enterprise plan, because that is the workspace the plan is checked against. The plugin reads
and changes data only in the workspace you choose here.

### 5. Review access, then Authorize

Check the requested permissions (see [What ChatGPT can access](#what-chatgpt-can-access)) and click
**Authorize**.

### 6. Click Try now

Back in ChatGPT you'll see **Plugin installed**. Click **Try now**.

### 7. Ask about your portfolio

ChatGPT starts a new chat with a prompt already tagged **@Kryptos**. Hit send:

```text
@Kryptos What is my crypto portfolio worth right now?
```

You're connected. ChatGPT answers with your holdings, cost basis and gains, straight from your Kryptos
workspace.

## Things to ask

Start any message with **@Kryptos** to route it to your Kryptos data:

- **Portfolio** — "What is my crypto portfolio worth right now?"
- **Transactions** — "Show me every swap I did in January" (search by asset, wallet, label or date)
- **DeFi and NFTs** — "Where am I staked, and how much am I earning?"
- **Data quality** — "Which of my transactions are missing prices or purchase history?"
- **Fixes** — "Recategorize all my Uniswap swaps as DeFi Swap" or "Add a manual BTC purchase from January 2024"
- **Tax** — "Summarise my realised gains and income for last tax year"
- **Accounting** — "Categorize my transactions and push the journals to Xero" (QuickBooks and SoftLedger
  work too)

## What ChatGPT can access

On the Authorize screen (step 5) Kryptos lists every permission the plugin asks for. Apart from your
email address, they cover only the workspace you chose in step 4.

| Area | View | Change |
|------|------|--------|
| Account | Your verified email address | — |
| Accounting | Accounting data and ledger entries | Create or modify accounting data and ledger entries |
| Contacts | Saved contacts and address book | Create, modify or delete contacts |
| Integrations | Integrations linked to your account | Connect, manage and modify integrations |
| Portfolios | Portfolios, holdings and balances | Create, modify or delete portfolios, holdings and balances |
| Reports | Generated reports | Create, modify or delete reports |
| Tax | Tax calculations and reports | Modify tax calculations and settings |
| Transactions | Complete transaction history | Create, update or delete transaction history |
| Workspace | Workspace settings and information | Manage users; modify workspace settings |

"Change" means records inside Kryptos, such as a label, a price or a manual trade. It never means moving
assets on an exchange or a blockchain.

## Troubleshooting

**"MCP access requires a Kryptos Pro membership or a paid Enterprise plan"**

- The workspace you picked in step 4 isn't on a paid plan. Upgrade it, or remove the plugin and install
  it again, choosing a workspace on Pro or a paid Enterprise plan.

**Kryptos doesn't answer, or ChatGPT answers without your data**

- Start the message with **@Kryptos** so ChatGPT uses the plugin.
- Open **Plugins** in ChatGPT and check that Kryptos shows as installed.

**A figure looks wrong**

- The plugin reports what is in your workspace, so the same figure shows in the Kryptos app.
  Ask *"@Kryptos Which of my transactions are missing prices or purchase history?"* to find the cause.
